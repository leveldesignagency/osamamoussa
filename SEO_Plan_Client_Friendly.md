# Getting Your Practice Found on Google

**A plan to help more patients find Mr Osama Moussa when they search for an Upper GI surgeon, acid reflux specialist, robotic surgery, or minimally invasive surgery in West London and the surrounding areas.**

---

## What We're Aiming For

When people in London and Hertfordshire search for things like “acid reflux doctor London”, “robotic surgery West London”, or “upper GI surgeon near me”, we want your website to appear as high as possible on Google. The plan below is how we get there.

**Where you are now:** Your website already has a strong foundation—good descriptions, your locations, and your expertise are in place. The main gap is that right now you have one main “Procedures” page that has to compete for every type of search. We fix that by giving each important topic its own page, so Google can show the right page to the right person.

---

### First batch – biggest impact

These five pages will help the most, so we do these first:

1. **Robotic surgery**  
   A dedicated page for robotic surgery in London and West London. This is a key differentiator and something many patients search for.

2. **Acid reflux / GORD**  
   A page focused on acid reflux and GORD treatment. Lots of people search for this; it matches your expertise and will bring in the right kind of enquiries.

3. **West London**

5. **LINX**

---

## Part 2: What Each New Page Needs

Every new page should be clear and useful for both patients and Google:

- **A clear main heading** at the top (e.g. “Robotic Surgery in London & West London”) that includes what people are searching for.
- **A short summary** (one or two sentences) that appears under your link in Google—mention the main topic, your location, and a reason to click (e.g. “Book a consultation”).
- **Several hundred words of helpful content** that explains the procedure or the area you serve. We’ll use phrases like “West London”, “minimally invasive”, “robotic surgery”, and “acid reflux” where they fit naturally.
- **Short subheadings** to break up the text (e.g. “What is robotic surgery?”, “How can I book?”) so it’s easy to read and so Google can show your page when people ask those questions.
- **Links to your Contact page and About page**, and to a few related procedures or area pages, so visitors can move easily around the site.

---

## Part 3: Small Improvements Across the Site

We don’t only add new pages; we also sharpen the wording on existing ones:

- **Homepage:** Make sure “West London” and “minimally invasive surgery” appear early (e.g. in the first line or two).
- **About page:** Add a short “Areas we serve” section (West London, Chelsea, Hertfordshire, Watford) so it’s obvious where you practise.
- **Contact page:** Mention West London, Hertfordshire, and your main procedures in the opening text.
- **Procedures page:** Add a short intro line above the list of procedures that includes “robotic surgery”, “acid reflux”, “minimally invasive”, and “West London”.

These are quick changes that help Google understand who you are and where you work.

---

## Part 4: How the Site Will Work Together

- **Homepage:** We’ll add a small “Key treatments” or “What we offer” section with links to the new procedure pages and the West London page.
- **Procedures page:** Each procedure card will link through to its own dedicated page (e.g. “Robotic Surgery” goes to the new robotic surgery page). The main Procedures page stays the place where everything is listed.
- **Each new page:** Will link to Contact, About, and to two or three related procedures or areas (e.g. the acid reflux page might link to Fundoplication, LINX, and Hiatal hernia).
- **Location pages:** Will list the hospitals you work at and how patients in that area can see you, with links to your procedures and Contact.

So: one topic per page, and clear paths between them. That’s what Google rewards.

---

## Part 5: Your Presence Outside the Website

What appears elsewhere on the internet also affects how you show up in search and in the “local” results:

- **Google Business Profile:** If you have (or can set up) a practice listing on Google, we make sure it’s complete—correct name, category (e.g. “General surgeon”), areas served, description (mentioning Upper GI, robotic surgery, acid reflux, West London), and photos. Reviews there help a lot.
- **Other listings:** We keep your name, address, and phone number exactly the same everywhere (Doctify, Top Doctors, hospital pages, any other directories). Consistency helps Google trust the information.
- **Reviews:** Encouraging patients to leave reviews on Doctify and on Google supports both your website and your local visibility. Your site already showcases reviews; we keep doing that.

---

## Part 6: Why This Works

- **One topic per page:** Instead of one page trying to rank for “robotic surgery”, “acid reflux”, “West London”, and everything else, we give each of those its own page. Google can then show the right page to the right search.
- **Location pages:** When someone searches “upper GI surgeon West London” or “acid reflux doctor Chelsea”, a page that’s specifically about that area is much more likely to appear.
- **Clear, trustworthy content:** We write in a way that shows your experience and expertise (e.g. your training, your hospitals, your research and reviews). Google favours content that genuinely helps people and comes from a credible source.
- **Linking between your own pages:** Linking from the homepage and Procedures page to the new pages—and between related procedures and areas—helps Google (and visitors) see how everything fits together and which pages are important.
