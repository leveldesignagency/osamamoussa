# SEO in place on osamamoussa.co.uk – Summary

**Purpose:** Evidence of the SEO work already implemented on the site. Use this when another party claims there is “no SEO” or that dedicated procedure/robotics pages are missing.

---

## 1. Dedicated procedure pages (14 pages, including Robotic Surgery)

- **Robotic Surgery:** `/procedures/robotic-surgery` – full page with title, description, hero, H1, expanded content, internal links.
- **Plus 13 other procedures:** Advanced Upper GI, Laparoscopic Surgery, Gallbladder/Cholecystectomy, GORD, Hernia Repair, LINX Device, Inguinal Hernia, TAPP Hernia, Epigastric Hernia, Ventral Hernia, Umbilical/Paraumbilical Hernia, Abdominal Wall Reconstruction, Skin Lumps/Lipomas.

Each procedure page has:

- Unique **page title** and **meta description** (for Google snippets).
- **Canonical URL** (so Google doesn’t treat duplicates as separate pages).
- **Open Graph** (title, description, URL) for sharing.
- **BreadcrumbList** structured data (Home → Procedures → [Procedure]).
- **MedicalWebPage** and **MedicalProcedure** structured data (so Google understands the page is about a specific medical procedure).
- Full content with **H1**, **H2** subheadings, and body copy.
- Links to Contact and “Other procedures” in the sidebar.

- **MedicalBusiness:** Practice name, description, URL, logo, image, phone, email, address, areas served (London, Chelsea, Belgravia, Fitzrovia, Holland Park, West London, Hertfordshire, Watford, Hemel Hempstead, Bushey, Northwood), medical specialties (Upper GI, robotic, laparoscopic, hernia, gallbladder, GORD, acid reflux, LINX, fundoplication, etc.), price range.

- **Organization:** Name, URL, logo, “sameAs” (ResearchGate, ScholargPS, I want great care, Top Doctors).
- **On every procedure page:** BreadcrumbList + MedicalWebPage + MedicalProcedure (as above).

## 4. Technical SEO

- **XML sitemap** at `https://www.osamamoussa.co.uk/sitemap.xml` including: homepage, About, Procedures hub, **all 14 procedure URLs**, Contact, Research, Reviews, Terms, Privacy. Priorities and change frequency set.
- **robots.txt:** Allows crawling of the site; disallows `/api/` and `/_next/`; points to the sitemap URL.
- **Google Search Console** verification meta tag in place (ownership verified for sitemap submission and reporting).
- **Favicon** and **Apple touch icon** for branding.

---

## 5. What “backlinks” means here

Backlinks are links **from other websites** (hospitals, Doctify, Top Doctors, directories, press) **to** this site. They are not stored in the code; they are earned via listings, partnerships, and content. The site is built so that when others link to it (e.g. to `/procedures/robotic-surgery` or the homepage), Google sees clear URLs, canonicals, and schema and can attribute value correctly. Building more backlinks is a separate marketing/outreach activity.
